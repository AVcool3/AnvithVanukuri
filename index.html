studiohone.com

Know your number.

StudioHone turns an investment opinion into a disciplined position size. You answer a handful of either-or questions to pin down a personal risk-aversion tier, state what you believe about a company — target, timeframe, confidence — and get back exact buy and sell weights with the reasoning for each one.

It blends your views with market equilibrium (Black-Litterman underneath), returns hedge guidance alongside the trades, and runs only against demo portfolios or Alpaca paper accounts. It can't move real money, on purpose.
